## Overview

Zoom is a widely recognized video conferencing platform that also offers webinar functionality. It leverages the same interface used for Zoom meetings, making it familiar to users, and is suitable for video-heavy webinars or those with multiple panelists.

## Key Features

- Up to 1,000 panelists and up to 50,000 attendees
- Mix of video, screen sharing, and streaming video of presenters
- Side-by-side views (e.g., presenter video alongside a shared screen demonstration)
- Audience questions in a pop-up window for presenters
- Streaming to Facebook, Workplace from Meta, YouTube, and Twitch
- Custom webinar names on Facebook and privacy settings on YouTube
- Automatic attendee registration and marketing campaign integration
- Zapier integration for automating workflows
- Templated events

## Pricing

- Webinar plans: From $89/month for up to 300 attendees and unlimited webinars
- Requires a paid Zoom Workplace Pro subscription, starting at $16.99/user/month

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS), with native apps for Web, Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android

## Notes

- Not a webinar-first tool; webinar features are part of a larger communications platform
