## Overview

Swoogo is a user-friendly event management platform designed for organizing in-person, virtual, and hybrid events. The platform emphasizes ease of use without sacrificing features, with a standout focus on customizable registration and event websites.

## Features

- Fully customizable registration forms with unlimited conditional logic and registrant types
- Drag-and-drop website builder for white-labeled event pages
- Customizable email marketing with automated email campaigns
- Native integrations with CRM tools, payment gateways, and video conferencing tools
- Mobile apps for iOS and Android
- Dashboard for tracking and measuring event success

## Pricing

No free plan available. Paid plans start at $11,800/year for one user and unlimited events and registrations.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (Web, iOS, Android)
