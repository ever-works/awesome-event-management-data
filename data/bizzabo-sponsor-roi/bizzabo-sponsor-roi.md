## Overview

Sponsor ROI is a sponsor management feature within Bizzabo's event management platform designed to help event organizers get sponsors active, connected to the right attendees, and ready to renew.

## Features

- Sponsor management
- AI-powered matchmaking between sponsors and attendees
- Lead retrieval
- Sponsor reporting and pipeline tracking
- Sponsor interaction tracking
- Floorplan upload with booth assignments and sponsor placements
- Task management for sponsor deliverables

Pricing is part of the Bizzabo platform; contact Bizzabo for details.

Cloud-based SaaS platform.

## Integrations

Integrates with the Bizzabo event management platform ecosystem.
