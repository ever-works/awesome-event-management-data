## Overview

eWebinar is a webinar automation platform that focuses exclusively on automating pre-recorded webinars on a schedule, complete with real-time interactions and live chat to deliver an engaging experience without requiring live hosting.

## Key Features

- Upload any video, webinar, presentation, or training; import from Zoom; or paste a link to videos hosted on YouTube, Vimeo, S3, and other providers
- Schedule webinars with start and end dates, days of the week, times, and blackout dates
- Just-in-time sessions where attendees can register and join in as little as five minutes
- Real-time chat with custom welcome and away messages
- Human-in-the-loop: hosts receive notifications when attendees respond and can continue the chat live
- Timeline-based interaction editor to drag and drop pre-populated questions, polls, downloads, tips, and offers at any point in the video
- Native integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, and ActiveCampaign
- Zapier integration for connecting with other apps
- In-depth webinar analytics

## Pricing

- Level 1 plan: $99/month for 1 active webinar, 1,000 registrants per month, unlimited team members
- Level 2 plan: $199/month for up to 5 published webinars and 3,000 monthly registrants

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS)

## Limitations

- No support for live webinars (pre-recorded and scheduled only)
