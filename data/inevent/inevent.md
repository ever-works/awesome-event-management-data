## Overview

InEvent is an event management platform focused on prioritizing the attendee experience. The platform provides travel and booking management for guests, along with AI-powered photo recognition capabilities for event imagery.

## Features

- Travel and booking management for guests
- AI-powered facial recognition tool to find attendees' photos across event images
- Attendee experience prioritization
- Event management tools
