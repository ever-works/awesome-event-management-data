## Overview

Wistia is primarily a video marketing platform that includes webinar functionality. It is positioned for enterprise, B2B, and account-based marketing organizations that need detailed audience analytics and content repurposing capabilities.

## Key Features

- Live or pre-recorded webinars
- Stream from external tools like OBS
- Simulcast to up to five destinations (with upgrade)
- Automatic content repurposing: social clips, highlight reels, and recaps
- Wistia Agent (Remix) for generating highlight reels, trailers, and compilations
- Full audience funnel analytics
- Breakdown of registrant organizations and employee counts per company
- Heatmaps of attendees, focused attendees, and chat activity overlaid on webinar video
- Individual attendee-level heatmaps with attendance, focus, and chat activity bars
- Marketing-focused integrations
- Zapier integration for automating workflows

## Pricing

- Webinar plans: From $537/month for up to 500 attendees and most features
- Webinars Plus plan: $1,037/month to simulcast to social, remove Wistia branding, and embed webinars on your own site
