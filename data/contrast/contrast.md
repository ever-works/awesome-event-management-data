## Overview

Contrast is a webinar platform designed for users who want visual customization of their webinars without needing the full video editing suite that Vimeo offers.

## Key Features

- Contrast Studio for previewing and customizing the look and feel of webinars
- Two themes to choose from
- Customizable brand colors and logos
- Animated topic introductions
- Automatic transcription of recordings
- In-depth analytics
- Quick clip creation for sharing on social media
