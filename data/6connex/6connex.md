## Overview

6Connex is an event platform known for its ease of use and flexibility, offering immersive virtual environments and a user-friendly interface. While some users note a steep learning curve on the back end, the platform provides strong customer service to assist with initial setup.

## Features

- Immersive virtual environments
- User-friendly interface
- Support for virtual and hybrid events
- Excellent customer service
