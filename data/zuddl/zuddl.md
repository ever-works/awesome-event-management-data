## Overview

Zuddl is a modular event platform that lets event managers build their own suite of features, paying only for the parts they need. The platform supports in-person, virtual, and hybrid events with a focus on immersive audience experiences and flexible feature scaling.

## Features

- Modular platform with scalable feature selection
- Landing page builder with SEO-friendly templates and custom-branded event websites
- Flow builder for complex ticketing flows with conditional branching
- Mobile app with chat, Q&A, and poll features for audience engagement
- Embeddable venue map and schedule builder
- 1:1 meeting scheduler for attendee networking
- Native integrations with Slack, HubSpot, and Stripe
- Zapier integration for connecting with additional tools

## Pricing

No free plan available. Paid plans start at $10,000 (billed annually).

## Deployment

Cloud-based (Web, iOS, Android)
