## Overview

Eventcombo is a comprehensive event management platform focused on marketing and promotion features. It provides tools for email campaigns, registration management, and attendee engagement across in-person, virtual, and hybrid events.

## Features

- Email Marketing Engine with drag-and-drop email builder supporting images, videos, and multimedia content
- Advanced email segmentation and scheduled delivery
- Email templates, A/B testing, and advanced analytics
- Customizable registration forms with conditional logic and nested fields
- Email domain approval and blocking for attendee management
- Automatic notifications for approved registrants
- Native integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, and Mailchimp
- Zapier integration for connecting with additional tools

## Pricing

Custom pricing (no free plan available).

## Deployment

Cloud-based (Web, iOS, Android)
