## Overview

Accelevents is an event management platform known for best-in-class customer support. The platform offers customization and flexibility for event registration, check-in, and website building, with integrations across CRMs and payment processors.

## Features

- Advanced registration workflows with conditional logic
- Automated check-in technology
- Website builder with dynamic widgets
- Native integrations with CRMs and payment processors
- Zapier integration for connecting with thousands of apps
- Mobile apps for iOS and Android

## Pricing

Paid plans starting at $7,000 for one event.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (Web, iOS, Android)
