## Overview

Zoho Webinar is a webinar platform offered as part of the Zoho suite of business applications. It provides a simple option for managing webinars without the extensive bells and whistles (integrations and analytics) of some other webinar apps, but is exceedingly affordable even at scale.

## Key Features

- Simple webinar management
- Affordable pricing that scales
- Part of the Zoho ecosystem
