## Overview

SpotMe is an enterprise-focused event management platform emphasizing security, compliance, and customer relationship management at scale. The platform provides control over event and attendee data hosting, with regular security assessments and deep CRM integrations.

## Features

- Robust security and compliance features
- Weekly vulnerability assessments and annual pentest reports
- Control over where event and attendee data is hosted
- Branded and secure event apps
- Personalized agendas, interactive polling, livestreaming, and post-event recordings
- Deep data insights flowing into CRM systems
- Support for simple webinars to complex, high-production events
- Zapier integration for connecting with organizational tools

## Pricing

Custom pricing (no free plan available).

## Deployment

Cloud-based (Web, iOS, Android)
