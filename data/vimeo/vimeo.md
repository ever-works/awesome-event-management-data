## Overview

Vimeo is a video hosting and editing platform that includes webinar capabilities. Its extensive video editing and post-production features make it suitable for users who want aesthetic control and powerful editing for webinar recordings.

## Key Features

- 10 professional templates with nine different scene options
- Fully customizable registration forms (aesthetics and fields)
- One-click "Go live" functionality
- In-event polls and Q&A sessions
- Live analytics: real-time watchers, peak viewers, total views, average time watched
- Robust post-event editing: trimming (including text-based editing), stitching videos, adding music, graphics, and text
- Turning clips into embeddable GIFs
- Customizable video player and end screen
- Captions, chapters, forms, and cards
- AI features: automatic transcript generation, subtitle translation, script generation, chapter creation, highlight reel cutting, audio dubbing, AI chatbot for viewer questions
- Zapier integration for automating workflows

## Pricing

- Advanced plan: From $125/month, required for webinar features
- Includes unlimited events with up to 100 attendees, interactive audience features, and event analytics

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS)
