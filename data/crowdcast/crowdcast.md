## Overview

Crowdcast is a webinar platform designed for affordability and scalability, supporting live events with up to 1,000 attendees. It is positioned as a cost-effective alternative to higher-priced webinar tools.

## Key Features

- Livestreaming to multiple services simultaneously (Facebook Live, YouTube, and any RTMP-supported destination including Twitch and LinkedIn Live)
- RTMP Mode for connecting to live streaming software like OBS, Wirecast, or Ecamm Live
- Paid webinars via Stripe or Patreon integration
- Accepting contributions during webinars
- Chat, polls, Q&A, and hand raising
- Clickable buttons for links and calls-to-action
- In-depth reporting with analytics on attendees, traffic, conversion rates, funnels, referrals, UTM tracking, and CTA analytics
- Zapier integration for connecting with other apps

## Pricing

- Lite plan: From $49/month for up to 100 live attendees and 10 hours of streaming per month
- Plans scale to up to 1,000 live attendees starting at $195/month

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS)
