## Overview

PheedLoop is an event management platform distinguished by its integrated Learning Management System (LMS) for courses and certifications. It supports community and learning management for events, with strong multi-language capabilities and dynamic virtual environment options.

## Features

- Integrated LMS for offering courses and certifications
- CE Credit Tracking with credit assignment to sessions and customized certificates
- Full-text support for more than 14 languages
- Live chat, gamification, and customizable virtual environments
- Seat and floor planning features for live events
- Dynamic virtual options for online events
- Zapier integration for automating repetitive tasks

## Pricing

Custom pricing (no free plan available).

## Deployment

Cloud-based (Web, iOS, Android)
